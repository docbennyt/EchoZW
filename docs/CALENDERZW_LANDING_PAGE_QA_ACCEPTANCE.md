# CalenderZW Landing Page QA + Acceptance

## Route regression

Verify `/`, `/find`, `/t/:slug`, `/admin`, `/admin/login`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/data-deletion`, `/support`, and active `/calendar/*` routes.

## Conversion

- CalenderZW identity visible immediately.
- Product purpose understandable in first viewport.
- Find my timetable dominant.
- See how it works secondary.
- Product proof demonstrates timetable → reminder/calendar value.
- No app/account friction communicated accurately.
- Admin visually secondary.
- Class-rep conversion appears after student value.

## Mobile matrix

Review 320×568, 360×800, 375×812, 390×844, 412×915, 430×932.

At each width verify: no horizontal overflow, readable logo, header fits, intentional hero wraps, CTA early, readable body, legible product scene, usable touch targets, motion not obscuring content, compact footer.

## Desktop matrix

Review 768, 1024, 1280, 1440, 1600+.

Verify intentional composition, readable max-widths, product visuals use available space, varied section rhythm, professional nav spacing, and no giant purposeless whitespace.

## Hero

- Headline directly explains value.
- Copy concise.
- Product visual specific to CalenderZW.
- CTA routes work.
- No fake metrics.
- Reduced-motion fallback exists.
- Hero still makes sense without motion.

## Content search

Search production-visible code for EchoZW, Echo Calendar, incorrect CalendarZW spelling, Lorem ipsum, placeholders, fake metrics, fake ratings, fake university claims, unverified Official labels, and outdated Google-sync claims.

## Google branding safety

Verify CalenderZW name, product purpose, aiDo operator relationship, Privacy, Terms, Data deletion, Support, metadata, canonical URL, and raw/no-JS identity where current architecture relies on it.

## Accessibility

Verify keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic heading order, landmarks, descriptive labels, reduced motion, no critical animation-only content, ~44px+ touch targets, and adequate contrast.

## Performance

No autoplay hero video, no WebGL, no unnecessary heavy media, reserved image dimensions, lazy-loaded below-fold media, no duplicate motion frameworks, bundle review, no major CLS, and page understandable with delayed JS.

## Motion

Reject constant floating decoration, scroll hijacking, animations delaying CTA, repeated bouncy cards, and layout-shifting effects. Preserve the signature timetable → reminder → calendar story and reduced-motion state.

## Visual consistency

Use one icon family, one radius language, one shadow language, one typography system, coherent button hierarchy, sparse gold, deep-green brand anchor, and avoid composing the entire site from rounded cards.

## Final questions

Would an unfamiliar student trust this from WhatsApp? Can they understand CalenderZW in five seconds? Is the main CTA unmistakable? Does mobile feel designed first? Does desktop feel intentionally recomposed? Is the product itself the hero? Does the page still feel uniquely CalenderZW? Did the redesign preserve routes/compliance? Did visual polish add unacceptable performance cost?
