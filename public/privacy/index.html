Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-05
Last updated: 2026-08-05

Operator: aiDo, operating the CalenderZW service. Privacy contact: privacy@aido.co.zw.

CalenderZW uses the minimum access needed to create and maintain a separate Google Calendar containing the timetable you choose. We do not read or modify your existing personal calendars.

This Privacy Policy explains how CalenderZW collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use our timetable discovery, calendar synchronisation, calendar subscription, reminder, administration, and related services. Where consent is legally required, we request it separately.

1. Scope

This policy applies to CalenderZW public timetable pages, administrator tools, calendar feeds, Google Calendar connection, downloadable calendar files, and support services operated from https://calender.aido.co.zw. Third-party services such as Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Microsoft Outlook, PesePay, and universities use their own policies and terms.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

We may collect account name and email address, institution/programme/cohort selections, timetable event information submitted by administrators, reminder preferences, support requests, correction reports, timetable evidence, payment references when paid features are enabled, and communications you send. Students do not need an account to view a public timetable or download a public calendar file.

2.2 Information collected automatically

We may collect device and browser type, operating system, approximate region, IP address in server/security logs, page views, interactions, referral source, diagnostics, timestamps, anonymous session identifiers, subscription identifiers, and feed retrieval timestamps for security, reliability, troubleshooting, measurement, and improvement.

2.3 Calendar and timetable information

We process selected timetables, course names and codes, lecture times, venues, lecturer names when included, semester dates, reminder settings, subscription state, and sync status to generate calendar files, subscriptions, and provider-specific events.

3. Google Calendar data

When you choose direct Google Calendar synchronisation, CalenderZW requests the Google OAuth permission required to create and manage a separate secondary calendar created by CalenderZW.

We use this permission only to create a dedicated timetable calendar, add selected timetable events, add reminder settings, update/cancel/remove CalenderZW-created events when timetables change, detect and recover from sync failures involving that calendar, and disconnect access when requested. CalenderZW does not use this permission to read, analyse, modify, or delete events from your pre-existing personal calendars.

The Google data we may store is limited to identifiers for the Google account when returned, the app-created calendar, app-created events, OAuth access and refresh tokens, sync timestamps, status/error information, and your selected timetable/reminders. Tokens are not sold, used for advertising, or used to build marketing profiles.

CalenderZW's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. CalenderZW does not use information obtained through Google Workspace APIs to develop, improve, or train generalised or non-personalised artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Humans do not access Google user data except when necessary to provide user-requested support with affirmative permission, investigate security or abuse, comply with law, or perform another use permitted by Google's applicable policies.

4. Apple Calendar, webcal, and downloads

Calendar applications may retrieve a private feed using an unguessable subscription URL. Anyone with that URL may be able to view the feed, so do not share it publicly. CalenderZW stores a cryptographic hash of feed tokens rather than raw tokens. Downloaded .ics files are controlled by you after download, and external calendar applications control refresh and notification delivery.

5. How we use information

We use information to display timetables, create downloads and subscriptions, create and maintain a CalenderZW Google calendar, apply reminders, publish corrections, authenticate users/admins, process reports, provide support, protect the service, diagnose failures, comply with law, process paid entitlements when enabled, and improve user-facing features. We do not sell personal information or Google user data. We do not use Google user data for targeted advertising.

6. Legal bases

Depending on your location and context, we process information because it is necessary to provide the service you requested, because you consented, because we have a legitimate interest in operating and protecting the service, or because we must comply with a legal obligation.

7. How information is shared

We share information with Google only when you initiate Google Calendar synchronisation, with your selected calendar application, and with service providers needed for hosting and operations. Current production processors should be confirmed by the operator before submission. CalenderZW does not share Google user data with advertising services.

8. Data retention

Public timetable versions and audit history may be retained for accuracy and accountability. Google tokens are retained only while direct sync remains connected. Event and calendar identifiers are retained while needed for synchronisation. Revoked feed tokens may be retained as non-usable hashes. Current configurable targets include 90 days for security logs, 30 days for deleted-account grace, 30 minutes for failed OAuth state, and 2555 days for payment records when payments are enabled.

9. Security

Current safeguards include HTTPS deployment requirements, restricted server-side credentials, secure SameSite cookies for anonymous sessions, hashed private-feed tokens, validation at API boundaries, dependency checks, and no token logging. Supabase RLS, encrypted refresh-token persistence, full audit logging, monitoring, and backups require production infrastructure configuration. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your choices and controls

You can avoid Google Calendar and use .ics instead, disconnect Google Calendar, revoke access through your Google Account, remove the app-created calendar, request private feed revocation, change reminders by creating a new subscription, request correction or deletion, and contact privacy@aido.co.zw. See Data deletion.

11. Deleting Google Calendar data

To disconnect Google Calendar, open account or calendar settings and select Disconnect Google Calendar. CalenderZW will revoke or discard stored OAuth credentials where present and stop future synchronisation. You may choose whether to keep or delete the secondary calendar before revocation. To request deletion of stored Google connection records, contact privacy@aido.co.zw from the email associated with the connection.

12. Account and data deletion

Instructions are available at /data-deletion.

13. Children and students

CalenderZW is intended for students and educational communities. It is not directed to children below the minimum age permitted to consent to online services in their location. Where required, an authorised adult must provide consent.

14. International processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than where you live. Where required, we use appropriate contractual, organisational, or legal safeguards.

15. Universities and timetable sources

Timetable information may be provided by institutions, administrators, class representatives, or verified community contributors. CalenderZW is not automatically the legal agent of every listed institution.

16. Changes

Material changes will be communicated through the service or by email where appropriate. Material changes to Google user-data use require renewed notice or consent where required.
