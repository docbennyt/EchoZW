CalenderZW support

Email support@aido.co.zw for help with timetable and calendar setup.

Calendar setup issues

Check that your reminder preset was saved, device calendar sync is enabled, and your calendar app has network access.

Google disconnect instructions

Use account settings to disconnect Google Calendar and choose whether to keep or delete the CalenderZW-created calendar. You can also revoke CalenderZW access in your Google Account.

Apple subscription guidance

Use the Apple Calendar subscription option from the live HTTPS site. Apple Calendar controls refresh timing.

.ics import guidance

Downloaded .ics files work for one-time imports. Future timetable updates may require a new download unless you subscribe.

Notification limitations

CalenderZW creates reminders, but final delivery depends on provider, phone settings, battery mode, connectivity, and permissions.

Report a timetable problem

Open the timetable page and choose Report a problem, or email the class link and correction to support.
