Terms of Service

CalenderZW helps students find a verified class timetable, choose useful reminders, and add lectures to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook, or another calendar application.

Google Calendar connection is optional. CalenderZW creates and manages a separate timetable calendar created by CalenderZW and does not read or modify your existing personal calendars.

Use CalenderZW responsibly and verify high-consequence timetable information with your institution.

Questions about these terms can be sent to support@aido.co.zw.
