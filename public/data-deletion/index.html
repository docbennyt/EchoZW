Data deletion

Effective date: 2026-08-05
Last updated: 2026-09-02

Use these options to disconnect calendar access, revoke private feeds, or request deletion of CalenderZW account and connection records.

1. Delete an account or records

Email privacy@aido.co.zw from the address associated with the account or connection. Explain what you want deleted and include any non-secret reference needed to locate the record. Do not send passwords, OAuth tokens, or private feed URLs in plain text.

2. Disconnect Google Calendar

After a direct Google Calendar connection succeeds, the public timetable shows a Disconnect Google Calendar control for that connection. Disconnecting revokes CalenderZW's Google authorization and deletes the encrypted refresh-token record stored by CalenderZW. You can also revoke CalenderZW through your Google Account's third-party connections.

Disconnecting does not silently delete a calendar you may want to keep. Where a deletion option is offered, you can explicitly ask CalenderZW to delete the secondary calendar it created. You may also contact support for help with revocation or deletion of connection records.

3. Revoke private feeds

A private subscription URL acts like a capability link. If it has been exposed or is no longer needed, request revocation using the supported settings flow or contact support with the relevant subscription reference. Do not publish the private URL itself.
