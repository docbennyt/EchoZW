CalenderZW by aiDo

Data deletion

Use this page to request deletion or disconnection of CalenderZW account, timetable, subscription, and Google Calendar connection records.

Delete an account

Use account settings when signed in, or email privacy@aido.co.zw from the email associated with the account. We display or send a confirmation reference after receiving the request.

Disconnect Google Calendar

Open account or calendar settings and select Disconnect Google Calendar. CalenderZW will revoke or discard stored OAuth credentials where present, mark the connection disconnected, and stop future sync. You can also remove CalenderZW access in your Google Account third-party connections settings.

Revoke private feeds

Private calendar subscription URLs can be revoked. After revocation, the feed returns not found and calendar clients stop receiving updates when they next refresh.

Request deletion without an account

Email privacy@aido.co.zw with the timetable, subscription reference, or contact details needed to locate your record. Some records may be retained for security, fraud prevention, accounting, legal compliance, or audit integrity.
